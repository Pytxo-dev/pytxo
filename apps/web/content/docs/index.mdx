Each agent's branch passes. Do they pass together? Pytxo merges your Claude Code, Codex and Copilot branches in a throwaway worktree, runs your tests on the combined result, and blocks the merge if it breaks. It is free, open source, and runs on your machine or in CI.

```bash
npx pytxo demo   # a throwaway repo where two agent branches pass alone and fail together
cd your-repo
npx pytxo        # checks your agent branches together
```

**Start here:** the [Quickstart](/docs/getting-started/quickstart) takes about a minute.

## What a verdict covers

Pytxo catches what your tests catch. A break your tests don't cover can still land, and merge conflicts are blocked, not resolved. The check runs your own test command; it calls no model and sends nothing anywhere.
