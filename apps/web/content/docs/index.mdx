Clear your agent PR pile in one command. Pytxo merges your Claude Code, Codex and Copilot branches in a throwaway worktree, runs your tests on the combined result, and lands the ones that pass. Every branch it leaves out gets the reason and a fix prompt to hand back to its agent. It is free, open source, and runs on your machine or in CI.

```bash
npx pytxo demo      # a throwaway repo: four agent branches, two land, two get the reason
cd your-repo
git fetch           # get the branches your agents pushed
npx pytxo           # checks your agent branches together
npx pytxo --apply   # lands the ones that pass
```

**Start here:** the [Quickstart](/docs/getting-started/quickstart). The demo lands a pile of four branches in under a minute; then you run the check on your own repository.

## What a verdict covers

Pytxo catches what your tests catch. A break your tests don't cover can still land, and merge conflicts are blocked, not resolved. The check runs your own test command; it calls no model and sends nothing anywhere.
