**Devin** (Cognition) sells a managed autonomous software engineer — Desktop, Cloud, review, integrations — Cognition’s agent, not your mixed CLIs ([devin.ai/pricing](https://devin.ai/pricing)).

**Amp** pushes remote, unsupervised “orbs” — scale when the laptop is closed ([ampcode.com](https://ampcode.com/)).

Pytxo’s bet is narrower and more plural: **coordinate the agents you already pay for** on local silicon (or your own sandboxes), with Race / Blast / Signal. It does not replace Devin or Amp as a managed remote engineer.

## Comparison

| Dimension | Devin / Amp | Pytxo |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Product | Managed / remote engineer | Local agent hypervisor |
| Who runs | Their agent identity | Your Claude / Codex / Cursor CLI / … |
| Laptop-closed | Core story | Optional; local-first by default |
| Keys | Product quotas / seats | BYOK. Your keys. Your silicon. |
| Collisions | Inside their product | Race Shield across your yard |

## When Devin / Amp fit

- You want to buy a remote engineer seat/quota
- Laptop-closed unsupervised scale is the requirement
- Single managed identity is simpler than operating CLIs

## When Pytxo adds value

- You already run (or will run) heterogeneous CLIs
- You want collision policy and approvals without vendor lock on the agent identity
- Structural Ops / Focus beats another chat-first remote product

Honest limit: if buyers want “ship PRs while I sleep” as a managed service, Devin/Amp/Oz win that pitch. Pytxo wins when the job is **hypervisor for what you already run**.
