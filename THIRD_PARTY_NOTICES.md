## Harness compatibility marks

Harness marks are shown only beside the corresponding vendor/product name to
identify a compatibility target. They do not imply installation, verification,
partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement. Full pinned paths, hashes, and
adaptations are recorded in `apps/desktop/public/ade/PROVENANCE.md`.

- **OpenAI**: exact black and white Mono Blossom SVGs from OpenAI's official
  2025 logo archive, used under the current OpenAI brand guidelines. OpenAI and
  its marks are owned by OpenAI.
- **Cursor**: exact dark/light 2D cube SVGs from Cursor's official brand kit.
  Cursor and its marks are owned by Anysphere, Inc.
- **Google Antigravity**: exact transparent full-color product icon from the
  official Antigravity press page. This local review asset is not cleared for
  public distribution; Google's current compatibility-icon process requires
  approval through Partner Marketing Hub.
- **Anthropic and Google Gemini**: vector paths from Simple Icons commit
  `4ba19240849175ab4b855a732ab98c0f87cfb714`. The Simple Icons collection is
  CC0-1.0, but its disclaimer notes that this does not waive third-party
  trademark or other rights in individual marks.
- **Factory Droid**: foreground geometry from Factory's official favicon; the
  solid favicon canvas was omitted for the transparent in-product treatment.
  Factory and Droid marks are owned by Factory AI.
- **Cline, goose, and Qwen Code**: artwork from the pinned official repositories
  listed in the provenance file. Those source repositories identify the
  relevant files under Apache-2.0.
- **Aider**: exact transparent favicon from `Aider-AI/aider` commit
  `5dc9490bb35f9729ef2c95d00a19ccd30c26339c`, used for source identification
  under Apache-2.0; Aider's mark remains its owner's property.
- **Grok Build**: exact light/dark transparent Grok logomarks from SpaceXAI's
  official asset archive, used without path, color, crop, or filter changes and
  only beside the Grok Build name under SpaceXAI's current brand terms.
